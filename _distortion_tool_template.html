Methodological note. This tool applies the Section 4 portfolio basis transfer — composition transfer followed by reserve-size scaling — to re-express historical market reserve movements on the user’s target portfolio basis. It is an upstream adjustment to the distributional input, not a tail model or capital-setting method. The actuary’s subsequent choice of distribution, fitting approach, and capital measure remains a separate modelling step.

How to Read This Tool

Purpose

This tool accompanies the paper Portfolio-Aware Scenario Transfer for Prior-Year Reserve Movements: Evidence from Lloyd’s Syndicates. It lets the reader apply the Section 4 portfolio basis transfer to an arbitrary target syndicate profile and inspect the resulting distributional shift.

What the tool does

For each historical donor syndicate-year in the eligible pool, the tool performs two sequential adjustments to the donor’s observed signed prior-year development (PYD) ratio:

  1. Composition transfer (Sraw → Smix). The donor’s line-level severity ratios are re-weighted to the target portfolio’s LoB composition, replacing the donor’s own premium mix.
  2. Reserve-size scaling (Smix → Sadj). The composition-transferred ratio is scaled by λ = √(Vsize(Rtarget) / Vsize(Rdonor)), where Vsize(R) = A + B·RC is the calibrated dispersion–reserve-size power law from Section 4.3.

The result is a pool of Sadj values that represent what the market’s historical PYD experience would have looked like had every donor been writing the target portfolio.

What the tool does not do

The adjusted distribution is an input to subsequent tail modelling, not a finished capital number. Choosing the distributional form, fitting method, and risk measure (e.g. VaR, TVaR) is the actuary’s responsibility and is outside the scope of this tool.

Interpreting the tabs

  • Distributions — KDE density of raw market vs target-basis signed PYD ratio, plus an empirical survivor function on the adverse (positive) tail. Compressing or spreading of the adjusted curve relative to the raw curve indicates the net effect of the basis transfer.
  • Statistics — Summary statistics (n, mean, standard deviation, Q75, VaR99, VaR99.5) for raw, composition-transferred, and fully adjusted distributions, with raw-to-adjusted deltas.
  • Waterfall — Shapley decomposition of the VaR99.5 movement into composition and reserve-size components. The two intermediate counterfactuals (mix-only, size-only) are averaged to produce path-independent attribution.
  • Donor Detail — Full donor table sorted by Sadj. Click any row to see the complete worked example: LoB-level projection table, Vsize calculation, and percentage changes at each step.
  • Data & Settings — Run provenance, donor eligibility rules, size-model coefficients, and formula version.

Donor eligibility

The donor pool matches the paper’s vignette sample: syndicate-years with LoB-level severity reconstruction, opening reserves ≥ £5m, a non-missing signed PYD ratio, and a RELIABLE or INCOMPLETE quality tag. This is the same pool used in the Appendix A worked vignettes.
