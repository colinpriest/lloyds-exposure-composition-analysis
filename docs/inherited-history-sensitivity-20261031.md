Execute `python src/targeted_inherited_history.py --output-dir NEW_DIRECTORY`; the directory must not already exist. The producer writes there only; it does not overwrite the historical 690 scenario.

The six unmodified producer output archives are committed under `results/inherited_history_draws/`, with their original filenames. The historical original-order 690 values are copied from the existing sensitivity result; that old result contains no ESS diagnostics. `docs/inherited-history-m01-proxies-20261031.json` records source amounts, currencies, conversion rates, pages and document hashes for the 11 M01 cases. `docs/inherited-history-source-locators-20261031.json` records the separate 41 source-audit amount locators. Numeric text/OCR matches alone do not establish that a number belongs to the correct column or provides an eligible original-year base.

The historical target is 690 = 666 + 24 selected eligibility-unresolved records. Its fit has 666 rows: 632 observed plus 10 unavailable outcomes and 24 hypothetical outcomes; 24 target observations with unavailable composition cannot be added to the fit. The expanded hypothetical target is 742 = 690 + 52, with 718 fitted rows. These additional records comprise 41 audited positive whole-account opening balances and 11 positive M01 source/model proxies. The 40 audited nil and 10 missing opening balances are excluded. The hypothetical outcomes, original-year eligibility, gross measurement and compatibility of those whole-account or transferred proxies are assumptions, not verified outcomes. In particular, 1980/2018 had nil before a $163.213m transfer whose application as a base is assumed; its existing False RITC flag is retained because the filing does not establish the registry definition of an inter-syndicate RITC. For 3902/2018 the exact source figure £30.259m replaces the model-rounded £30.3m. Raw audited USD values are converted using reporting-year closing USD per GBP; GBP values are unchanged. This sensitivity is neither an exhaustive population nor a probability bound.

Evenly spaced unit Student-t quantiles are assigned in ledger order for the original scenario. This deterministic placement assigns differing magnitudes across record groups and is not an identified missing-outcome distribution. The alternative reassigns the same quantiles with `numpy.random.default_rng(20261031).permutation(n)` and uses the same placement at c=1 and c=5. Both use unit likelihood weights. It is one specified alternative rather than an exhaustive permutation analysis.

All six new fits had zero divergences, maximum R-hat at most 1.00463, minimum bulk ESS 1,298 and minimum tail ESS 1,428. The c=5 expanded intervals abut the prior support boundary k=0.5. The 690 c=5 mean moved by +0.02514 under the stated permutation, showing that its single scenario number is sensitive to synthetic-outcome placement. The clean producer rerun reproduced all six independently executed fit summaries and full diagnostics exactly; its numerical model inputs were also compared independently. This check does not establish robustness over all assignments or the empirical eligibility of the hypothetical cases.
