\begin{minipage}{0.9\textwidth}\small
Dual-model output is available for 948 of 1065 source files; pre-model stubs and single-model files are outside that statement. Those files were processed by Gemini 2.5 Flash and GPT-5 Mini. The key field compared was \texttt{prior\_year\_development\_pct}. A disagreement was classified as material when the absolute difference exceeded 0.5\,pp. This is the pack's own measure of how far the two readings stood apart, not the extraction pipeline's field comparator, which flags a numeric field at 0.5\% of the larger and 0.05 absolute. Of 948 dual-model files, 38 exhibited material disagreement. Which reading this analysis carries forward is decided in the loader and not by this measure: where the extraction's own validation passed, it is the first model in name order; where it did not, it is the model with the higher recorded \texttt{prior\_year\_movement\_confidence} among those that give a figure. Readings the extraction itself could not reconcile are queued in its field-level disagreement log: 542 entries remain pending review, including 201 from prompt 2.13, while 545 entries carry a resolved status. The 38 pack-threshold file count above is a separate descriptive measure and is not an adjudication count.
