\midrule
Pooling rule & Event groups with fewer than 3 syndicate-year observations are merged into a year-level pooled group (\texttt{\{year\}\_pooled}). This ensures each group has sufficient mass for within-group variation to be estimable in the RE-GLS model. \\

Minimum count & $n_{\min} = 3$. Groups with $n < 3$ are pooled. \\
