## The question

development table. For each of the three reports decide, from the pages: which axis carries the underwriting years and
which the development ages; whether the printed labels match the body; and what the table's cells are for each
underwriting year at each year end. Two readings so far disagree on the 2024 table: one reads it as printed with its
labels transposed (rows are underwriting years), the other reads it as labelled (columns are underwriting years). Both
agree the 2023 table is transposed.

2. Tie each underwriting year's cells across the reports: the same underwriting year's cell at the same year end should

   labelled, and transposed) against the 2022 and 2023 reports.
3. Any liability or outstanding row, cumulative paid row, or total in the table, and which orientation it ties to
   (reserve = cumulative claims - paid, per underwriting year, against the balance sheet and notes).
4. Any statement in the text about an underwriting year's loss ratio or ultimate (for example a 2021 ultimate gross
   loss ratio at the year end) and which orientation agrees with it.
5. The net table, if printed, and whether its orientation agrees.
6. Verdict for the 2024 table: `as_labelled` or `transposed` or `undeterminable`, with the decisive evidence; then the
   2024 primary outcome M = sum over underwriting years u <= 2022 of (end-2024 cell - end-2023 cell), in USD 000, with the
   cells; and the same for the 2023 report (u <= 2021) as a check.
