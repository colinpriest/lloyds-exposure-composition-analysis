# Source-history rule: census adjudication (decisions of the Claude analysis session, 26 October 2026)

Rule (recommendation of the Codex analysis, adopted on Colin's instruction of 26 October 2026, and written into the
analysis's rules before any refit): set aside an observation when specific source evidence raises a material unresolved
concern about overlapping claims history, gross/net mixing or cohort reassignment through closure, and the declared gross
original-cohort outcome cannot be defensibly reconstructed or validated; regardless of the outcome's size or sign; an
unexpired exposure, ordinary internal RITC, funds-withheld arrangement, unusual business, youth or SPA status is not
sufficient by itself; a calendar/triangle inequality alone is insufficient where FX or accounting scope differs.
The concern was found during Codex's tail investigation (6131/2021); the census applied it to every record its
source-feature criteria select (`population.md`: 81 core, 20 host-side; chosen without outcome values).

Readings: round 9, `D:/tmp/r62/oc/readings/brief_r9.md`, two independent arms K1-K8 and L1-L8 (every record read
twice); third readings Z11 (6118/2018), Z12 (3330/2017, 3330/2018), Z13 (3500/2019, 3000/2016) where the facts
conflicted. A decision rests on facts at least two readings report.
