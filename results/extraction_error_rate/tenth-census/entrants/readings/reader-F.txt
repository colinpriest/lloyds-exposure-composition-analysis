6. RECONCILIATION WITH THE COMMENTARY AND THE NOTES
============================================================
Commentary (E5). The three amounts the models summed are printed in STERLING ("£21.3m", "£12.8m",
"£5.4m"), in a report whose statements are in USD. The models' notes call them "GBP"; gemini then
assumed they were USD. So the models' 39.5 is £39.5m entered as $39.5m. At the report's own
conversions (the same page gives "£29.3m or US$36.6m", ratio 1.249; Note 26 end-of-period GBP 0.80,
i.e. 1.25 USD per GBP), £39.5m is about US$49.4m (inference: 39.5 x 1.25 = 49.38).
Scope of those amounts: healthcare "The 2022 and prior gross reserves were increased by £21.3m";
US General Liability "£5.4m for the 2022 and prior years"; aviation war "£12.8m gross", no years
stated, but the portfolio "was discontinued in 2022" (so 2022 and prior, inference). The 2022 year
of account is the closing year, so "2022 and prior" is the paper's cohort u <= t-2 exactly.
The commentary names these as the "three drivers" of the 2022 year of account's loss; it does not
state a total prior-year movement, and it says of healthcare "We have increased reserves on all
years" (so part of that driver also sits in 2023-2024).
Against the triangle: about US$49.4m of the +US$77.9m on u <= 2022 is itemised (63%); about
US$28.5m is not (inference, by difference). The steps are adverse in every year 2014-2022 and
largest in 2021 (+25.1m) and 2022 (+24.8m), then 2017 (+8.1m), 2020 (+7.2m) and 2019 (+6.0m),
which fits a healthcare book written "since 2016" strengthened "on all years" and a US GL book
discontinued in 2020. The filing does not split the unexplained remainder. A possibility the filing
neither confirms nor quantifies: part of UW 2022's step at its second year-end could be late
earning of binder or risks-attaching business; the paper's rule counts u = t-2 in full, so this is
interpretation, not an error.
The difference between the triangle and the commentary sum is not itself an error (clarification,
point 3): the commentary is a partial, sterling itemisation, not the movement for u <= 2022.

Notes. The notes print no prior-year line (E4). Note 4's "Expected cost of current year claims"
(314,005) is the year's entire gross claims incurred (150,649 paid + 163,356 change in provision),
all underwriting years together, so it cannot be split into current and prior years. The triangle's
2024 step over all years (77,918 + 86,265 + 148,515 = 312,698) reconciles with that 314,005 to 0.4%
(single end-2024 rate in the table, transaction rates in the accounts), and to 36 thousand if the
one out-of-line 2014 cell is taken at the 2023 report's figure (section 5; inference).
