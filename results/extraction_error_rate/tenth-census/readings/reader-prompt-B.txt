- A take-on is another syndicate's liabilities brought in during the report year (by reinsurance to close (RITC), a quota share of another syndicate's older years, or a portfolio transfer). Where a triangle is restated to carry the take-on, or a provisions note's prior-year line follows a take-on row, the take-on is added to R instead (main.tex ~2541-2549). A syndicate-year that accepts another syndicate's older liabilities, effective in the report year, is placed in a separate "RITC regime". A syndicate's internal RITC of its own years of account is not a take-on.
