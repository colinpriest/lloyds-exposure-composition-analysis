Transferring 491 market donor observations onto the target portfolio basis decreases the adverse tail of the signed PYD ratio distribution. The largest absolute change occurs at VaR99 (0.5636 raw vs 0.3909 adjusted). The size adjustment dominates the distortion at VaR99.5 (concentration effect 0.0136, size effect 0.0302). The donor pool comprises 491 observations with 285 adverse outcomes. Tail support: 5 observations beyond VaR99, 3 beyond VaR99.5 on the adjusted distribution. The profile transition from Pre-exit diversified syndicate (£800m) to Post-exit concentrated syndicate (£650m) reflects Exit of Marine after weak profitability. These results confirm that naive pooling of market reserve movements would misrepresent the syndicate's 1-in-200 reserve risk.
