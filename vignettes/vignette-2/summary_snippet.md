Transferring 789 market donor observations onto the target portfolio basis decreases the adverse tail of the signed PYD ratio distribution. The largest absolute change occurs at VaR99 (0.7666 raw vs 0.4022 adjusted). The size adjustment dominates the distortion at VaR99.5 (concentration effect 0.0113, size effect 0.0402). The donor pool comprises 789 observations with 453 adverse outcomes. Tail support: 8 observations beyond VaR99, 4 beyond VaR99.5 on the adjusted distribution.
