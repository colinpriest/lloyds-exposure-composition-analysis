Transferring 726 market donor observations onto the target portfolio basis decreases the adverse tail of the signed PYD ratio distribution. The largest absolute change occurs at VaR99 (0.7660 raw vs 0.3856 adjusted). The size adjustment dominates the distortion at VaR99.5 (concentration effect -0.0705, size effect -0.1939). The donor pool comprises 726 observations with 430 adverse outcomes. Tail support: 8 observations beyond VaR99, 4 beyond VaR99.5 on the adjusted distribution. For this target and this donor library, the comparison illustrates how naive pooling of market reserve movements would misstate the transferred 1-in-200; it does not establish the target syndicate's true 1-in-200 reserve risk. Basis: these figures apply the size and concentration scale ratio only. The manuscript's headline stresses additionally apply the RITC tail-regime quantile map, so they are not the same quantity and will not match (V1 VaR99.5 is 0.393 there).
